Overview
Private Limited Company Registration in Noida
A Private Limited Company is the structure most Noida founders choose — and for good reason. The city's IT and SaaS start-ups raise angel and VC money, its electronics and export units need bank credit and corporate customers, and both are far easier with a company than with a proprietorship or partnership. Investors, in particular, will rarely fund anything else.
Registration is fully online through the MCA's Central Registration Centre, but the details that trip people up are local: the correct Uttar Pradesh stamp duty, an address proof that exactly matches your Noida sector and PIN code, and an NOC from the right property owner. We handle all of it and typically have your Certificate of Incorporation in 7–10 working days.
Noida specifics
What is specific to Noida
ROC Kanpur
is your Registrar. Incorporation is central, but every later filing — AOC-4, MGT-7, DIR-12, strike-off — goes to Kanpur.
Uttar Pradesh stamp duty
applies to the MoA, AoA and SPICe+ form. For small authorised capital it is generally lower than Delhi's capital-linked rate.
Registered office in a Noida sector or Greater Noida West society
is fine — a recent PVVNL or NPCL bill, the owner's NOC and a rent agreement (if rented) are all you need.
Noida Authority or YEIDA allotted premises:
the allotment or lease deed serves as ownership proof; the NOC must come from the allottee.
After incorporation
your GST registration falls under the Uttar Pradesh jurisdiction, and Shops & Establishment registration under the UP Act if you employ staff.

Can two friends living in Noida register a Pvt Ltd company from home?
Yes. Two individuals can be both the directors and the shareholders, and a residential flat in Noida can be the registered office with the owner's NOC and a recent utility bill.
I work in a Sector 62 IT company. Can I be a director of my own Pvt Ltd company?
The Companies Act does not prohibit it, but most employment contracts restrict directorships or outside business. Check your contract, or consider having a family member as director until you leave.
Will my Noida company be able to raise funding from investors?
Yes — this is the main reason to choose a Private Limited Company. It can issue equity and preference shares, convertible notes and ESOPs, which LLPs and OPCs cannot. We draft the Articles with future funding rounds in mind.
How long does it take in Noida?
Typically 7–10 working days from complete documents, the same as anywhere in India, because incorporation is processed centrally. Name approval is usually the variable step.
