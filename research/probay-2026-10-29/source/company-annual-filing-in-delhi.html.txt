Overview
Company Annual Filing in Delhi
Companies registered in the National Capital Territory of Delhi file their annual returns with the Registrar of Companies, Delhi. Every year the company must hold its AGM by 30 September, file audited financial statements in AOC-4 and the annual return in MGT-7 or MGT-7A, and keep the deposit return and director KYC current — irrespective of turnover or activity.
Delhi has one of the largest populations of registered companies in India, and ROC Delhi is correspondingly active in issuing notices, adjudicating penalties and striking off defaulting companies. Our team keeps your Delhi company clean on the MCA portal: accounts, audit coordination, board and AGM documentation, filings and responses to any notice.
Delhi specifics
What is specific to Delhi
Filed with ROC Delhi
(NCT of Delhi & Haryana), which issues notices, adjudication orders and strike-off proceedings for Delhi companies.
AGM to be held in Delhi
(the city of the registered office) or by video conferencing where permitted; for Section 8 and larger companies additional rules apply.
Companies with foreign investment
— common in Delhi — must also file the annual FLA return with the RBI and comply with FEMA reporting, which we track alongside the ROC calendar.
Delhi companies holding property or investments
often have DPT-3 and MSME-1 obligations that are overlooked; we include them in the health check.
Non-operating Delhi companies
can be closed by strike-off after regularising filings up to the year business ceased — usually cheaper than continued compliance.

FAQs
Company Annual Filing in Delhi — common questions
What are the annual filing due dates for a Delhi company?
AGM by 30 September; AOC-4 within 30 days of the AGM; MGT-7/7A within 60 days of the AGM; DPT-3 by 30 June; DIR-3 KYC as notified by the MCA.
We received a notice from ROC Delhi for non-filing. Can you help?
Yes. Send us the notice. We assess the pending filings and penalties, prepare a reply, complete the filings and, where appropriate, apply for compounding or respond in adjudication proceedings.
What does annual filing cost for a Delhi Private Limited Company?
From ₹4,999 professional fee for AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A with audited accounts, plus MCA filing fees. Complete annual packages start at ₹9,999. Statutory audit is billed separately by an independent Chartered Accountant.
Our company has an NRI shareholder. Is there anything extra?
Possibly. Foreign investment triggers FEMA reporting — the FC-GPR at the time of allotment and the annual FLA return to the RBI — in addition to the ROC filings. We check and include what applies.
Can the AGM be held online?
Companies can hold the AGM through video conferencing where the MCA has permitted it by circular for the relevant year; otherwise it must be held at the registered office or elsewhere in Delhi. We confirm the applicable rule each year.
