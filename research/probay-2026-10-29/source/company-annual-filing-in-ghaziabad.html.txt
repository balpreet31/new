Overview
Company Annual Filing in Ghaziabad
Ghaziabad companies — whether a manufacturer in Sahibabad, a distributor in Raj Nagar or a consultancy in Indirapuram — file their annual returns with the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur. The obligation is the same every year: hold the AGM, file the audited financial statements in AOC-4 and the annual return in MGT-7 or MGT-7A, and keep director KYC and the deposit return current.
We see two typical situations in Ghaziabad. Active family-run companies want a reliable team to handle the accounts, audit coordination and ROC filings without chasing. And older companies that were formed years ago and left unfiled need a clear plan: file the backlog with additional fees, or strike the company off. We give you the numbers for both and handle whichever you choose.

FAQs
Company Annual Filing in Ghaziabad — common questions
What are the due dates for a Ghaziabad company's annual filing?
AGM by 30 September; AOC-4 within 30 days of the AGM; MGT-7/7A within 60 days of the AGM; DPT-3 by 30 June; DIR-3 KYC as notified by the MCA.
Our company in Ghaziabad has bank loans. Does that change the filing?
The filings are the same, but the financial statements must correctly disclose secured borrowings, and any charge registration (CHG-1) or satisfaction (CHG-4) must be up to date on the MCA portal, since banks and auditors verify it.
What does annual filing cost?
From ₹4,999 professional fee for AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A, plus MCA filing fees. Full-year packages including DPT-3, DIR-3 KYC, resolutions and registers start at ₹9,999. Statutory audit is separate.
The company was formed in 2019 and never filed anything. What should we do?
Send us the CIN. We check the status, estimate the additional fees for each pending year, and compare that with the cost of striking the company off. Many owners choose closure; if you want to keep the company, we regularise it.
Can you attend to ROC Kanpur notices for us?
Yes. We draft and file replies to notices and adjudication proceedings and, where needed, represent the company before the Registrar.
