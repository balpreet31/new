Overview
One Person Company Registration in Noida
Freelancers, consultants, solo developers, Amazon and Flipkart sellers and independent professionals across Noida often outgrow the proprietorship model: clients want to contract with a company, banks want a corporate borrower, and the owner wants personal assets protected. A One Person Company gives all of that with a single owner-director.
Incorporation is online through the MCA. For a Noida OPC we take care of Uttar Pradesh stamp duty, the nominee's consent in Form INC-3, a matching address proof for your Noida flat or office and the post-incorporation steps — bank account, INC-20A and, if needed, Uttar Pradesh GST registration.
Noida specifics
What is specific to Noida
ROC Kanpur
is your Registrar after incorporation; the OPC files AOC-4 and the simplified MGT-7A there every year.
Uttar Pradesh stamp duty
on the MoA, AoA and SPICe+ form, generally modest for small capital.
Your Noida residence
can be the registered office with the owner's NOC and a recent PVVNL or NPCL bill.
The nominee
— usually a spouse or parent — need not live in Noida; they sign INC-3 and provide KYC.
No AGM is required
for an OPC, which keeps annual compliance lighter than a regular private company.

FAQs
One Person Company Registration in Noida — common questions
How much does OPC registration cost in Noida?
Professional fee from ₹999, plus one Digital Signature, MCA fees and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty based on authorised capital. Itemised written quote before payment.
Is an OPC better than a proprietorship for a Noida freelancer?
If you invoice corporate clients, want limited liability or plan to hire, yes. A proprietorship is cheaper to run but offers no liability protection and less credibility. The OPC does require a statutory audit and annual ROC filings, which we handle.
Can I later convert my Noida OPC into a Private Limited Company?
Yes, at any time, by adding a second member and director and altering the MoA and AoA. The earlier compulsory conversion thresholds no longer apply.
Who can be my nominee?
Any Indian citizen who is a natural person and has consented in Form INC-3 — most owners choose a spouse, parent or sibling. The nominee can be changed later.
How long does it take?
Typically 7–10 working days from complete documents, the same as a Private Limited Company.
