Overview

LLP Registration in Delhi

Delhi's professional firms — chartered accountants, lawyers, architects, consultants, designers and agencies — as well as many trading and service partnerships choose the LLP structure. It gives limited liability, a separate legal identity and freedom to set profit sharing and management in the agreement, with no board meetings, no AGM and no mandatory audit below the thresholds.

Registration is done online with the MCA. For a Delhi LLP the details that matter are the stamp duty on the LLP Agreement under Delhi rates, an address proof for the Delhi registered office in the right owner's name, and Form 3 filed within 30 days. We handle all three, and can also convert an existing Delhi partnership firm into an LLP.

Delhi specifics

What is specific to Delhi

  • ROC Delhi is your Registrar for Form 11, Form 8 and every change in partners or agreement.
  • LLP Agreement on Delhi stamp paper — the duty depends on the partners' contribution; the agreement is filed in Form 3 within 30 days.
  • Any Delhi premises — office, flat or co-working desk — can be the registered office with the owner's NOC and a recent BSES or Tata Power-DDL bill.
  • Professional practices should check their regulator's rules on multi-disciplinary or LLP practice before finalising the name and objects.
  • Delhi GST and Delhi Shops & Establishment registration follow incorporation where applicable.

Checklist

Documents required in Delhi

For each partner

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence
  • Latest bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number

Delhi registered office

  • Latest electricity bill (BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna or Tata Power-DDL), gas, telephone or mobile bill — not older than 2 months
  • NOC from the property owner
  • Rent agreement, if rented or in a co-working space
  • Sale deed, DDA allotment letter or MCD property tax receipt, if self-owned
Step by step

LLP Registration process in Delhi

Digital Signature Certificates Day 1–2

Class-3 DSCs for the designated partners via Aadhaar eKYC.

Name reservation — RUN-LLP Day 2–5

Name and trademark check, then reservation with up to two name choices.

Incorporation filing — FiLLiP Day 5–9

FiLLiP filed with partner details, Delhi registered office proof and consents; DPIN allotted.

Certificate of Incorporation Day 9–12

COI with LLPIN, plus the LLP's PAN and TAN.

LLP Agreement & Form 3 Within 30 days

Agreement drafted and executed on Delhi stamp paper of the correct value, then filed in Form 3.

Bank account & registrations After COI

Current account, and GST or MSME registration if needed.

FAQs

LLP Registration in Delhi — common questions

What does LLP registration cost in Delhi?

Professional fee from ₹1,499, plus Digital Signatures, MCA fees based on contribution and Delhi stamp duty on the LLP Agreement. You receive an itemised written quote first.

Can a CA or law firm in Delhi register as an LLP?

Yes — LLPs are widely used by professional firms. Each profession's regulator has its own rules on names, partners and permitted activities, which we check before filing.

Can we convert our Delhi partnership firm into an LLP?

Yes, under the LLP Act, provided all partners of the firm become partners of the LLP. We handle the conversion and the follow-up intimation to the Registrar of Firms.

How is the stamp duty on the LLP Agreement calculated in Delhi?

It is based on the total capital contribution of the partners under Delhi's stamp schedule. We compute it for your contribution and include it in the quote.

How long does LLP registration take in Delhi?

Typically 10–15 working days from complete documents, plus execution and filing of the LLP Agreement within 30 days of incorporation.
