Why Ghaziabad

Ghaziabad: NCR's manufacturing and trading powerhouse

Ghaziabad is one of the largest industrial cities in Uttar Pradesh. Sahibabad, Bulandshahr Road and Meerut Road industrial areas host thousands of units in steel, electronics, engineering goods, textiles and packaging, while Indirapuram, Vaishali, Vasundhara and Raj Nagar Extension have become home to a fast-growing base of consultants, traders, e-commerce sellers and start-ups.

The Delhi–Meerut Expressway, the Namo Bharat (RRTS) corridor and Delhi Metro's Blue and Red lines put Ghaziabad minutes from Delhi at far lower rents. A large number of Ghaziabad businesses still operate as proprietorships or unregistered partnerships — converting to a company or LLP is often the single step that opens the door to bank credit, corporate customers and government tenders.

Ghaziabad-specific rules

What is different about registering a company in Ghaziabad?

1. ROC Kanpur has jurisdiction

Ghaziabad district is part of Uttar Pradesh, so your company or LLP falls under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur. Incorporation is processed by the MCA's Central Registration Centre; every later filing — annual returns, director changes, charges, strike-off — goes to ROC Kanpur.

2. Uttar Pradesh stamp duty

Stamp duty on the incorporation form, MoA and AoA is charged at Uttar Pradesh rates and paid online through the MCA portal. For small authorised capital it is generally lower than the Delhi rate, where duty on the AoA is linked to the amount of capital. We calculate the exact figure for your capital before filing.

3. Address proof in industrial and GDA properties

Many Ghaziabad premises are on plots allotted by the Ghaziabad Development Authority or UPSIDA, or in older colonies where the electricity connection is in a family member's name. The MCA needs the address proof, the NOC and the form to match exactly. We check the ownership chain and prepare the NOC from the right person so the application is not sent back for resubmission.

4. Converting an existing firm

If you already run a proprietorship or partnership in Ghaziabad, we can either incorporate a fresh company and transfer the business, or convert a partnership firm into an LLP or company under the Companies Act. The right route depends on your assets, GST registration and contracts — we advise on a call.

Trans-Hindon or Ghaziabad city? It does not matter for the ROC — both are in Ghaziabad district, Uttar Pradesh. It only matters for your GST jurisdiction and municipal registrations, which we also handle.

Checklist

Documents required for company registration in Ghaziabad

Directors & shareholders

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence
  • Latest bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number

Ghaziabad registered office

  • Latest electricity bill (PVVNL), gas, telephone or mobile bill — not older than 2 months
  • NOC from the property owner
  • Rent agreement, if rented
  • GDA allotment letter, sale deed or Nagar Nigam property tax receipt, if self-owned

Electricity bill in a relative's name? That is fine — the person named on the bill gives the NOC. If the bill is in the name of a deceased owner, we will need the succession documents or a different proof, so tell us early.

FAQs

Company registration in Ghaziabad — common questions

How much does it cost to register a company in Ghaziabad?

Our professional fee starts at ₹999 for a Private Limited Company or OPC and ₹1,499 for an LLP. Digital Signatures, MCA fees and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty are extra and depend on authorised capital. We send an itemised written quote before you pay — or try the cost calculator for an indicative figure.

Which ROC covers Ghaziabad?

Ghaziabad district is in Uttar Pradesh, so companies and LLPs registered here come under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur.

Can I use my flat in Indirapuram or Vaishali as the registered office?

Yes. A residential address is acceptable as the registered office. You need a recent electricity or utility bill for the flat, an NOC from the owner, and a rent agreement if the flat is rented.

I run a manufacturing unit in Sahibabad as a proprietorship. Should I convert to a company?

If you supply to corporates, want bank credit against the business, or plan to bring in partners or investors, a Private Limited Company or LLP is usually worth it. We can incorporate a new entity and help transfer the business, or convert a partnership firm directly. The best route depends on your assets and GST position — we advise on a free call.

Is a factory licence or trade licence needed after incorporation?

Incorporation with the MCA does not replace local licences. Depending on your activity you may need a Factory Licence, UP Shops & Establishment registration, a Nagar Nigam trade licence, pollution consent or an MSME (Udyam) registration. We tell you which apply and can arrange them.

Do I need to visit any office in Kanpur or Lucknow?

No. The MCA process is entirely online. Documents are signed with a Digital Signature and the certificate arrives by email.
