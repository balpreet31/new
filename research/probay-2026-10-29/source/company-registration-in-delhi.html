Why Delhi

Delhi: the capital's credibility for your company

Delhi is the commercial and administrative heart of North India. Consultancies and law firms around Connaught Place, IT and electronics businesses in Nehru Place and Okhla, exporters in Mohan Cooperative and Naraina, traders in Chandni Chowk and Karol Bagh, and a huge base of start-ups and professionals across Dwarka, Rohini, Laxmi Nagar and South Delhi all operate here.

Delhi-specific rules

What is different about registering a company in Delhi?

1. ROC Delhi has jurisdiction

Companies with a registered office in the National Capital Territory of Delhi fall under the Registrar of Companies, Delhi, which also covers Haryana. Incorporation is processed by the Central Registration Centre; annual filings, event-based forms and strike-off applications are dealt with by ROC Delhi.

2. Delhi stamp duty is linked to authorised capital

Stamp duty on the MoA, AoA and SPICe+ form is charged at Delhi rates and paid online through the MCA portal. In Delhi, duty on the Articles of Association is linked to authorised capital, so choosing an unnecessarily high authorised capital increases the cost of incorporation. Most founders start with ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh authorised capital and increase it later when funding is actually raised. We calculate the exact duty for your capital before filing and show it in your quote.

3. Address proof in Delhi's property landscape

Delhi has DDA flats, cooperative group housing societies, freehold and leasehold plots, MCD-assessed shops and a great many rented premises. The MCA needs a recent utility bill in the name of the owner, an NOC from that owner, and a rent agreement where applicable. Using a home address as the registered office is permitted; if you plan to run manufacturing or a shop from the premises, local zoning rules apply separately.

4. Delhi versus UP: choose deliberately

Because Noida and Ghaziabad fall under a different state and ROC, shifting your registered office across the border later needs Regional Director approval and takes months. If your team and clients are split across NCR, tell us — we help you pick the state that fits your plans, not just today's address.

Delhi Shops & Establishment registration is separate from MCA incorporation and is generally required once you employ staff at a Delhi premises. We can obtain it along with GST and MSME registration after your certificate is issued.

Checklist

Documents required for company registration in Delhi

Directors & shareholders

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence
  • Latest bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number

Delhi registered office

  • Latest electricity bill (BSES Rajdhani, BSES Yamuna or Tata Power-DDL), gas, telephone or mobile bill — not older than 2 months
  • NOC from the property owner
  • Rent agreement, if rented or in a co-working space
  • Sale deed, DDA allotment letter or MCD property tax receipt, if self-owned

Co-working or virtual office in Delhi? Acceptable, provided the operator gives you a licence agreement, an NOC and a recent utility bill for the premises. Some operators charge a separate fee for the NOC — ask before you sign.

FAQs

Company registration in Delhi — common questions

How much does company registration cost in Delhi?

Our professional fee starts at ₹999 for a Private Limited Company or OPC. You also pay for Digital Signatures, MCA government fees and Delhi stamp duty, which rises with authorised capital. We give you an itemised written quote first; the cost calculator shows an indicative figure for Delhi instantly.

Which ROC does a Delhi company come under?

The Registrar of Companies, Delhi, whose jurisdiction covers the NCT of Delhi and Haryana.

Is Delhi stamp duty higher than Noida or Ghaziabad?

For small authorised capital the difference is modest; for larger capital Delhi's duty on the Articles of Association, which is linked to the capital amount, can be noticeably higher than the fixed Uttar Pradesh rate. We show both figures if you are undecided between the two states.

Can I register my company at a DDA flat or a rented home in Delhi?

Yes. A residential address can be the registered office. You need a recent utility bill in the owner's name, the owner's NOC and, for rented premises, the rent agreement.

Can I register in Delhi and operate from Gurugram or Noida?

Legally the company must keep its registered office at the address filed with the ROC and receive communication there. You can have branch or operating offices elsewhere. But if your entire team sits in Noida, registering there may save you a state-shift application later — we help you weigh it.

Do I need to visit the ROC office at IFCI Tower?

No. Incorporation and all subsequent filings are done online through the MCA portal. Physical visits are only needed in rare cases such as hearings, which we attend on your behalf.
