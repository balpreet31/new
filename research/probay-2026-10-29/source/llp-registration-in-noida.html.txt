Overview
LLP Registration in Noida
Noida's consultants, architects, designers, IT service providers, CA and law practices and family-run service businesses often prefer an LLP: it gives limited liability and a separate legal identity like a company, but without mandatory audit below the thresholds, board meetings or an AGM. Two professionals sharing an office in Sector 18 or a team of freelancers in Sector 62 can run an LLP with very little ongoing paperwork.
The incorporation is processed online by the MCA. The local details — a Noida address proof that matches exactly, Uttar Pradesh stamp paper for the LLP Agreement, and Form 3 filed within 30 days — are what make the difference between a clean registration and months of resubmissions. We take care of all of it.

FAQs
LLP Registration in Noida — common questions
What is the cost of LLP registration in Noida?
Professional fee from ₹1,499, plus Digital Signatures, MCA fees based on contribution and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty on the LLP Agreement. We give an itemised written quote first.
LLP or Pvt Ltd for a Noida IT services business?
If you will raise investment or want to issue ESOPs, choose a Private Limited Company. If you want low compliance, no mandatory audit below ₹40 lakh turnover or ₹25 lakh contribution, and flexible profit sharing, an LLP fits. We help you decide on a free call.
How long does LLP registration take in Noida?
Typically 10–15 working days from complete documents, followed by execution and filing of the LLP Agreement within 30 days of incorporation.
Can the LLP's registered office be a Greater Noida West society flat?
Yes, with a recent utility bill, the owner's NOC and, if rented, the rent agreement.
Does a Noida LLP need an audit?
Only if its turnover exceeds ₹40 lakh or its contribution exceeds ₹25 lakh in the financial year. Below these limits, only Form 11, Form 8 and the income tax return are required each year.
