AOC-4, MGT-7 and ADT-1 are counted from the actual AGM date — hold the AGM earlier and the filings fall due earlier. Where a due date falls on a holiday, file before it; the MCA portal does not extend automatically.

MCA extensions are announced by General Circular, usually in October or November, and apply only to the forms and period named. Do not plan on an extension.

Not on the calendar

Deadlines triggered by events

  • Within 15 days: ADT-1 after appointing an auditor; PAS-3 after a rights issue or private placement allotment.
  • Within 30 days: DIR-12 for director appointment or resignation; INC-22 for change of registered office; SH-7 for increase in authorised capital; MGT-14 for resolutions required to be filed; CHG-1 for creation or modification of a charge; CHG-4 for satisfaction; Form 3 and Form 4 for LLP agreement and partner changes.
  • Within 60 days: share certificates after allotment; the first board meeting and auditor appointment within 30 days of incorporation.
  • Within 180 days: INC-20A declaration of commencement of business after incorporation.

Monthly rhythm

Recurring monthly and quarterly dates

  • 7th of every month — deposit of TDS deducted in the previous month.
  • 11th and 20th of every month — GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for monthly filers (quarterly filers under QRMP: 13th and 22nd/24th of the month after the quarter).
  • 15th of the month — PF and ESI contributions for the previous month.
  • 31 July, 31 October, 31 January, 31 May — quarterly TDS returns (24Q / 26Q).
  • 31 December — GSTR-9 annual return for the previous financial year.
FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date for AOC-4 and MGT-7 in 2026?

If the AGM is held on the last permitted day, 30 September 2026, AOC-4 is due by 29 October 2026 and MGT-7/7A by 28 November 2026. Earlier AGM, earlier due dates.

What are the LLP filing due dates in 2026?

Form 11 by 30 May 2026 and Form 8 by 30 October 2026, for the financial year ended 31 March 2026.

When is DIR-3 KYC due?

By 30 September each year for every person holding a DIN as on 31 March. Missing it deactivates the DIN, which is restored on filing with a ₹5,000 fee.

Does a newly incorporated company have to file all of these in its first year?

A company incorporated on or after 1 January may close its first financial year on 31 March of the following year, so its first AGM and annual filings come a year later. INC-20A, auditor appointment and the first board meeting apply from day one.

Is DPT-3 required if the company has no deposits?

It is required if the company has any outstanding loan or receipt of money not treated as a deposit — including director loans — as on 31 March. A company with nothing outstanding need not file.
