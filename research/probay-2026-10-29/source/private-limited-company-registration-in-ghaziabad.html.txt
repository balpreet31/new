Overview
Private Limited Company Registration in Ghaziabad
Ghaziabad's manufacturers, traders and distributors increasingly need a Private Limited Company rather than a proprietorship: corporate buyers ask for it, banks lend more readily against it, and it protects family property from business liabilities. The city's newer consultancies, e-commerce sellers and start-ups in Indirapuram and Raj Nagar Extension choose it for the same reasons, plus the ability to raise investment.
The process is online through the MCA's Central Registration Centre. What we add is local know-how — Uttar Pradesh stamp duty calculated for your capital, address proof sorted out for GDA or UPSIDA-allotted properties, and a clean transfer plan if you are moving an existing business into the new company. Certificate of Incorporation usually arrives within 7–10 working days.

Private Limited Company Registration in Ghaziabad — common questions
What does it cost to register a Pvt Ltd company in Ghaziabad?
Professional fee from ₹999, plus Digital Signatures, MCA fees and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty based on authorised capital. We give you an itemised written quote; the
cost calculator
shows an indicative total.
Can I convert my Ghaziabad proprietorship into a Private Limited Company?
A proprietorship is not converted as such — a new company is incorporated and the business, assets and GST registration are transferred to it. We plan the transfer so that there is no gap in invoicing or bank operations.
Can the registered office be my factory in Sahibabad Industrial Area?
Yes. You need the UPSIDA lease or allotment document, a recent electricity bill and an NOC from the allottee. If the plot is leased to a family member or another firm, the NOC comes from them.
Do I need a separate trade or factory licence?
MCA incorporation does not replace local licences. A manufacturing unit may need a Factory Licence, pollution consent and MSME (Udyam) registration; a shop or office may need UP Shops & Establishment registration. We list what applies and can arrange them.
Is there any advantage of registering in Ghaziabad rather than Delhi?
For most small businesses the practical advantages are lower rent, Uttar Pradesh MSME incentives and, for larger authorised capital, lower stamp duty. If your customers, team and premises are in Ghaziabad, register in Ghaziabad — shifting the registered office across states later is slow and expensive.
