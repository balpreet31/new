Overview
Company Annual Filing in Noida
Companies with their registered office in Noida, Greater Noida or Noida Extension fall under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur. Every year — regardless of whether the company traded — they must hold an Annual General Meeting, file the audited financial statements in Form AOC-4 and the annual return in Form MGT-7 or MGT-7A, and keep the director KYC and other periodic forms current.
Late filing costs ₹100 per day per form with no upper limit, and two years of default can lead to the company being struck off and its directors disqualified. Our CA/CS team handles the whole cycle for Noida companies: accounts, audit coordination, board and AGM paperwork, and the ROC filings — with reminders well before every due date.

FAQs
Company Annual Filing in Noida — common questions
What are the annual filing due dates for a Noida company?
AGM by 30 September; AOC-4 within 30 days of the AGM; MGT-7 or MGT-7A within 60 days of the AGM. DPT-3 by 30 June and DIR-3 KYC as notified. The MCA occasionally extends dates by circular.
Our Noida company had no transactions this year. Do we still need to file?
Yes. Nil-activity companies must still hold the AGM and file AOC-4 and MGT-7A. If the company will never operate, consider our
strike off
service instead.
What does annual filing cost for a small Noida company?
From ₹4,999 professional fee for AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A when accounts are already audited, plus MCA filing fees. Complete packages including DPT-3, DIR-3 KYC and minutes start at ₹9,999. The statutory audit is billed separately by an independent CA.
We have not filed for two years. What now?
We check the company's status on the MCA portal, estimate the additional fees, prepare the pending accounts and file year by year. If the ROC has already issued a strike-off notice, we respond to it as part of the process.
Can you also handle our GST and income tax returns?
Yes. Our Premium plan covers bookkeeping, financial statements and the income tax return, and we can add GST return filing for Noida businesses.
