Overview

One Person Company Registration in Ghaziabad

Many Ghaziabad shopkeepers, traders, contractors, e-commerce sellers and consultants run their business single-handedly as a proprietorship. A One Person Company lets a single owner operate through a proper company — with personal assets protected, a corporate name on invoices and easier access to bank credit and corporate customers — without needing a partner or a second shareholder.

Registration is fully online. For a Ghaziabad OPC we handle the Uttar Pradesh stamp duty, the nominee's consent, address proof for your shop, flat or godown in the district, and the steps after incorporation — bank account, INC-20A, GST and MSME registration.

Ghaziabad specifics

What is specific to Ghaziabad

  • ROC Kanpur is the Registrar for all filings after incorporation, including the simplified annual return MGT-7A.
  • Uttar Pradesh stamp duty on the MoA, AoA and SPICe+ form.
  • Shop or residence in Raj Nagar, Kavi Nagar, Indirapuram or Vaishali can be the registered office with the owner's NOC and a recent PVVNL bill.
  • Traders and sellers usually need Uttar Pradesh GST registration immediately after incorporation; e-commerce sellers need it regardless of turnover.
  • MSME (Udyam) registration for a Ghaziabad manufacturing or trading OPC unlocks priority-sector lending and UP scheme benefits.

Checklist

Documents required in Ghaziabad

For the member & the nominee

  • PAN card and Aadhaar card
  • Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence
  • Latest bank statement or utility bill (not older than 2 months)
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Email ID and mobile number

Ghaziabad registered office

  • Latest electricity bill (PVVNL), gas, telephone or mobile bill — not older than 2 months
  • NOC from the property owner
  • Rent agreement, if rented
  • GDA allotment letter, sale deed or Nagar Nigam property tax receipt, if self-owned
Step by step

One Person Company Registration process in Ghaziabad

Digital Signature Certificate Day 1–2

Class-3 DSC for the member-director via Aadhaar eKYC.

Name reservation — SPICe+ Part A Day 2–4

Name and trademark check; the name must end with "(OPC) Private Limited".

MoA, AoA & nominee consent Day 3–5

e-MoA, e-AoA, nominee consent in INC-3 and director declarations drafted.

Filing SPICe+ Part B Day 5–7

Incorporation filed with DIN and AGILE-PRO-S; MCA fees and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty paid online.

Certificate of Incorporation Day 7–10

COI with CIN, PAN and TAN by email.

Bank account & commencement After COI

Current account at a Ghaziabad branch and INC-20A within 180 days.

FAQs

One Person Company Registration in Ghaziabad — common questions

What is the cost of OPC registration in Ghaziabad?

Professional fee from ₹999, plus one Digital Signature, MCA fees and Uttar Pradesh stamp duty based on authorised capital. We send an itemised written quote first.

I sell on Amazon from Ghaziabad. Should I register an OPC?

An OPC gives you a company name, limited liability and better standing with marketplaces and suppliers. You will also need GST registration, which is mandatory for e-commerce sellers — we arrange both.

Can my father be the nominee even though he lives in Meerut?

Yes. The nominee can live anywhere in India; they need to be an Indian citizen and sign the consent in Form INC-3.

Does an OPC need an audit even if turnover is small?

Yes. Statutory audit is mandatory for every company regardless of turnover. It is one of the costs to weigh against a proprietorship; we include audit coordination in our annual filing plans.

How long does OPC registration take in Ghaziabad?

Typically 7–10 working days from complete documents.
