FAQs

Questions founders ask us most

How long does company registration take in Delhi, Noida or Ghaziabad?

A Private Limited Company is typically incorporated in 7–10 working days once all documents and Digital Signatures are ready. The timeline depends on name approval and processing by the MCA's Central Registration Centre, which handles incorporation for all states.

What is the total cost of registering a Private Limited Company?

The total cost has four parts: our professional fee (from ₹999), Digital Signature Certificates for directors, MCA government fees, and state stamp duty. Stamp duty differs between Delhi and Uttar Pradesh and also depends on your authorised capital. We give you an exact, itemised written quote before you pay.

Can I use my home address as the registered office?

Yes. A residential address can be used as the registered office. You will need a recent utility bill (electricity, gas, telephone or mobile — not older than 2 months) and a No Objection Certificate from the property owner. A rent agreement is also required if the premises are rented.

Do I need to visit your office or the ROC?

No. The entire process is online. You share documents over WhatsApp or email, sign using your Digital Signature, and receive the Certificate of Incorporation by email. We are happy to meet in person in Delhi NCR if you prefer.

Which ROC has jurisdiction over Noida, Ghaziabad and Delhi?

Companies with a registered office in Noida, Greater Noida or Ghaziabad fall under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur (Uttar Pradesh). Companies registered in Delhi fall under the ROC, Delhi (NCT of Delhi & Haryana). New incorporations are processed centrally by the Central Registration Centre.

Private Limited Company or LLP — which one should I choose?

Choose a Private Limited Company if you plan to raise equity funding, issue ESOPs or scale quickly — investors strongly prefer it. Choose an LLP if you are a professional or service firm that wants limited liability with lower compliance and no mandatory audit below the prescribed turnover and contribution thresholds.

What compliances are required after incorporation?

Key post-incorporation steps include opening a bank account, depositing subscription money, filing INC-20A (commencement of business) within 180 days, appointing the first auditor, holding board meetings and an AGM, and filing AOC-4 and MGT-7/7A every year along with income tax returns.

Do you serve clients outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. Since MCA filings are fully online, we register companies and handle compliance for clients across India. Our on-ground focus and in-person support is for Delhi, Noida and Ghaziabad.
