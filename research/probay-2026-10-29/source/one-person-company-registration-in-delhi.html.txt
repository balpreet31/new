Overview
One Person Company Registration in Delhi
Delhi's independent consultants, designers, lawyers-turned-entrepreneurs, boutique traders and solo technology founders use the One Person Company to operate as a company without a co-founder. It gives limited liability, a separate legal identity and the credibility a Delhi corporate or government client expects — while the owner keeps 100% of the shares and all decisions.
Incorporation is online with the MCA. For a Delhi OPC we take care of Delhi stamp duty (linked to authorised capital), the nominee's consent in INC-3, a matching address proof for your Delhi flat or office, and the post-incorporation steps such as bank account, INC-20A and Delhi GST registration.

FAQs
One Person Company Registration in Delhi — common questions
How much does OPC registration cost in Delhi?
Professional fee from ₹999, plus one Digital Signature, MCA fees and Delhi stamp duty based on authorised capital. Itemised written quote before you pay.
Can an NRI register an OPC in Delhi?
Yes. Since 2021 an Indian citizen who is not resident in India can incorporate an OPC. Documents executed abroad need notarisation and apostille.
Can I run a consultancy from my South Delhi flat as an OPC?
Yes. A residential address can be the registered office. For a consultancy with no walk-in customers, this is the most common set-up.
Is an OPC taxed differently from a proprietorship?
Yes. An OPC is a company and pays corporate income tax on its profits; the owner is taxed separately on salary or dividends. Whether this is better than proprietorship slab rates depends on your income — we can run the comparison.
How long does it take?
Typically 7–10 working days from complete documents.
