Noida-specific rules
What is different about registering a company in Noida?
The Companies Act is the same across India, but three practical things change when your registered office is in Noida or Greater Noida rather than Delhi.
1. Your ROC is Kanpur, not Delhi
Noida falls in Gautam Buddha Nagar district of Uttar Pradesh, so your company comes under the
Registrar of Companies, Kanpur
. New incorporations are processed centrally by the MCA's Central Registration Centre, but all later matters — annual filings, event-based forms, notices and strike-off — are handled by ROC Kanpur.
2. Uttar Pradesh stamp duty applies
Stamp duty on the incorporation form, MoA and AoA is charged at Uttar Pradesh rates and paid online through the MCA portal. For companies with small authorised capital, UP stamp duty is generally lower than Delhi's, where duty on the AoA rises with authorised capital. We calculate the exact figure for your capital before filing.
3. Your address must say Uttar Pradesh
It sounds obvious, but a surprising number of applications are sent back because the address proof, NOC and form show mismatched details — "Noida, Delhi NCR", missing sector numbers, or an old district reference. We verify that the utility bill, NOC and SPICe+ form carry the identical address, with the correct district and PIN code.
Thinking of shifting later?
Moving your registered office from Noida to Delhi (or the reverse) is a change of state, which requires Regional Director approval through Form INC-23. It is far easier to choose the right state at incorporation — ask us if you are unsure.

Which ROC does a Noida company fall under?
Noida and Greater Noida are in Gautam Buddha Nagar district of Uttar Pradesh, so companies and LLPs registered here fall under the Registrar of Companies, Kanpur. Incorporation itself is processed online by the MCA's Central Registration Centre.
Can I register a company at my flat in Noida or Greater Noida West?
Yes. A residential flat can be your registered office. You need a recent electricity or other utility bill for the flat and an NOC from the owner. If it is rented, a rent agreement is also needed.
I live in Noida but my business is in Delhi. Where should I register?
The registered office should be where you can reliably receive official communication and keep statutory records. Many founders use their Noida home address initially. Keep in mind that shifting the registered office between Uttar Pradesh and Delhi later requires Regional Director approval, so it is worth deciding carefully — we can advise on a call.
Do I have to visit Kanpur or any government office?
No. The full process is online through the MCA portal. Documents are signed digitally and the Certificate of Incorporation is issued by email. No visit to Kanpur, Lucknow or any office is required.
Can you help with GST registration and a bank account in Noida after incorporation?
Yes. We assist with current account opening at your preferred bank, GST registration under the Uttar Pradesh jurisdiction, MSME (Udyam) registration and Shops & Establishment registration where applicable.
